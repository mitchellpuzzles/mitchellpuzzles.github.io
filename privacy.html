Marble Regions ("the app") is made by Daniel Mitchell ("we"). This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

Your game data stays on your device

Your progress, statistics and settings are stored only on your phone. We have no accounts and no servers that collect your game data. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow it), your IP address (used to estimate a general location), information about the ads you see and tap, and diagnostic data. On iPhone, the app asks for permission before your advertising identifier can be used for tracking, and you can change this in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. In regions where it is required, the app shows a consent form, and you can change your choice any time in the app's Settings under "Privacy choices for ads". See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases (Apple, Google and RevenueCat)

If you subscribe to Marble Regions Plus, the payment is handled by Apple or Google, and you manage or cancel the subscription in your App Store or Google Play account. We use RevenueCat to confirm your purchase. It receives an anonymous app identifier and your purchase history, but never your payment details. See the RevenueCat privacy policy.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, it is scheduled on your phone. No information is sent to us.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. Questions: mitchell.puzzles@gmail.com.
